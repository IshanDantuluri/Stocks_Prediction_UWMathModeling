## Next experiment

Do not add trees to the existing combined model. `gated_news_residual.py` now
implements the next experiment:

1. Preserve the frozen quant-only prediction.
2. Generate expanding-year (2018-2022) out-of-fold quant predictions with
   five-session boundary purging.
3. Train strongly regularized Ridge corrections only on news-active OOF rows,
   with each active session receiving equal total training weight.
4. Apply exactly zero correction to inactive rows.
5. Select Ridge strength and correction scale using 2023-2024 only; scale zero
   is a candidate, so no correction can win.
6. Treat 2025 as exploratory because it has already been inspected.
7. Cache each expensive OOF fold atomically and emit 30-second fit heartbeats.
8. Save all validation candidates, hashes, feature/prompt contracts, paired
   diagnostics, and the exact inactive-row invariant.

Run (long command; user-run):

```bash
python3 gated_news_residual.py
```

- `gated_news_residual_5d_rank.joblib` — selected residual artifact
- `gated_residual_paired_daily.csv` — paired validation/test diagnostics

## Model-behavior audit

The apparent lack of signal was partly a modeling problem:

- The 100-tree baseline has in-sample annual IC around 0.10-0.24 but collapses
  after 2022, demonstrating substantial historical interaction overfit.
- Several simple range/volatility features retain positive univariate IC in
  2023-2026 even when the tree's aggregate prediction fails.
- Only about 3% of the joint tree's split gain comes from news.
- It makes **zero ticker-news splits**. `min_samples_leaf=200` and extreme
  ticker-event sparsity prevent company-specific news from entering the tree;
  almost all news gain is from sector broadcasts.

`quant_univariate_ic_audit.csv`.

## Recommended next data work

Do not add trees or a larger neural network to the current inputs. The next
material improvements require new point-in-time information:

1. Historical earnings surprise versus consensus, analyst estimate revisions,
   fundamentals, and guidance changes.
2. Point-in-time S&P membership to remove survivor/constituent-selection bias.
3. Reliable article timestamps so after-close news can be separated from
   intraday news.
4. Much broader ticker-news recall and source coverage; resolving the current
   extra-name list alone recovers only a small number of exact missing
   candidates.
5. Options/implied-volatility or short-interest data if historically
   point-in-time and affordable.

Generic sentiment is not enough: the model needs **surprise relative to market
expectations**. Any new source must be stored with its as-of timestamp and joined
only after its publication delay.

## Per-sector specialist ablation completed 2026-07-23

`sector_specialist_walkforward.py` implements the final hierarchical model
experiment:

1. retain the frozen 20-session quant+SEC prediction for global/sector
   allocation;
2. train eleven independent Ridge models on within-sector feature and target
   ranks;
3. center specialist predictions within each date/sector; and
4. add them to the centered global rank using one shared validation-selected
   weight from 0, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, or 1.

Training remains annual walk-forward with an eight-year trailing window and
purges labels crossing each refit boundary. Specialist alphas 1,000, 10,000,
and 100,000 were evaluated using 2023–2024 only.

Validation selected specialist weight **0**. The control IC/spread was
0.05769/2.289%; the best positive blend (alpha 1,000, weight 0.1) fell to
0.05626/2.191%. Larger weights deteriorated monotonically. Reporting therefore
remains exactly the global model: 2025 IC/spread 0.0340/1.664% and
partial-2026 0.0333/3.306%.

Sector-balanced diagnostics reinforce the rejection:

- specialist-only IC was 0.0074 in 2023, -0.0087 in 2024, -0.0009 in 2025,
  and 0.0174 in partial 2026;
- a 10% blend produced sector-balanced IC 0.0319, -0.0184, 0.0116, and
  0.0300 respectively, versus global 0.0354, -0.0182, 0.0121, and 0.0286;
- apparent sector advantages were unstable. For example, Energy's validation
  mean specialist lift was positive but reversed in 2025, while Information
  Technology became strong only in partial 2026.

Artifacts:

- `sector_specialist_20d_2026.joblib`;
- `sector_specialist_20d_predictions.csv`;
- `sector_specialist_diagnostics.csv`;
- `portfolio_sector_specialist_20d_2025_summary.json`; and
- `portfolio_sector_specialist_20d_2026_summary.json`.

The conclusion is not that sector behavior is identical. It is that the

sector corrections. More specialization would increase variance and tuning
degrees of freedom without a validation basis.
